I am a Ph.D. candidate in Management with a focus on **Big Data Management** at the Southwestern University of Finance and Economics. From 2025 to 2026, I am a government-sponsored visiting Ph.D. student at Nanyang Technological University, Singapore.

My research interests include **federated learning, data privacy protection, reinforcement learning, and time-series clustering**. I am particularly interested in personalized and efficient distributed learning under real-world data and device heterogeneity.

For a complete and current publication list, please visit my [Google Scholar profile](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=pJW7tXcAAAAJ).
