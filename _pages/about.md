I am a Ph.D. candidate in Management with a focus on **Big Data Management** at the Southwestern University of Finance and Economics. From 2025 to 2026, I am a government-sponsored visiting Ph.D. student at Nanyang Technological University, Singapore.

My research interests include **Personalized Federated Learning, LLMs-based Unlearning, LLMs-based Agent and Security**. I am particularly interested in personalized and efficient distributed learning under real-world data and device heterogeneity.

For a complete and current publication list, please visit my [Google Scholar profile](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=pJW7tXcAAAAJ).

# 📝 Publications

- [**SeqFedEDT: Accelerating Sequential Federated Learning on Non-IID Data via Element-Wise Decoupled Training**](https://www.computer.org/csdl/journal/tm/2026/05/11263984/2bPscEOegW4), **Tian Du**, Xingyan Chen, Mu Wang, Yaling Liu, Su Yao, Gang Kou, Fuzhen Zhuang, Changqiao Xu, Gabriel-Miro Muntean, *IEEE Transactions on Mobile Computing*, 2026.
  <br> SeqFedEDT speeds up sequential federated learning on non-IID data by decoupling model parameters at the element level to better balance global sharing and local adaptation.

- [**FedOBP: Federated Optimal Brain Personalization through Cloud-Edge Element-wise Decoupling**](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.16574), Xingyan Chen, **Tian Du**, Changqiao Xu, Fuzhen Zhuang, Lujie Zhong, Gabriel-Miro Muntean, Enmao Diao, *arXiv preprint*, 2026.
  <br> FedOBP personalizes federated models by identifying important parameters through quantile-based element-wise decoupling, improving the trade-off between shared knowledge and client-specific adaptation.

- [**Clustered Federated Learning with Cloud-Edge Personalized Model Decoupling**](https://doi.org/10.11897/SP.J.1016.2025.00407), **Tian Du**, Xingyan Chen, Gang Kou, Yu Zhao, Changqiao Xu, *Chinese Journal of Computers*, 2025.
  <br> This paper proposes a clustered federated learning method for cloud-edge systems, using personalized model decoupling to address client heterogeneity and improve collaborative training.

- [**Energy-Efficient Model Decoupling for Personalized Federated Learning on Cloud-Edge Computing Networks**](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1002/ett.70203), Chutong Jin, **Tian Du**, Xingyan Chen, et al., *Transactions on Emerging Telecommunications Technologies*, 2025.
  <br> This work reduces the energy cost of personalized federated learning in cloud-edge networks through adaptive model decoupling and layer-wise personalization.

- [**Towards Optimal Customized Architecture for Heterogeneous Federated Learning with Contrastive Cloud-Edge Model Decoupling**](https://doi.org/10.1109/TC.2024.3514302), Xingyan Chen, **Tian Du**, Mu Wang, Tiancheng Gu, Yu Zhao, Gang Kou, Changqiao Xu, Dapeng Oliver Wu, *IEEE Transactions on Computers*, 2025.
  <br> FedCMD dynamically selects personalized layers for heterogeneous federated learning, using contrastive cloud-edge model decoupling to improve convergence and model performance.

- [**Time Series Clustering Based on Relationship Network and Multidimensional Feature Fusion**](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.eswa.2022.119481), **Tian Du**, et al., *Expert Systems with Applications*, 2023.
  <br> This paper improves time-series clustering by modeling relationships among series and fusing multidimensional features to capture richer temporal structure.

- [**Multivariate Time-Series Clustering Based on Component Relationship Networks**](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.eswa.2021.114649), Hailin Li, **Tian Du**, *Expert Systems with Applications*, 2021.
  <br> This paper clusters multivariate time series by building component relationship networks that capture correlations across variables more explicitly.
